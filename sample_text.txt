Last summer, Sarah decided to travel across Europe with her best friend Emma. They started their journey in Paris, where they visited the Eiffel Tower and enjoyed delicious French pastries every morning. The weather was absolutely wonderful, and the city felt magical at night. From there, they took a train to Berlin, a city famous for its rich history and vibrant nightlife. Sarah loved the street art and the friendly locals, although she found the public transportation system a bit confusing at first. Their next stop was Rome, where they explored ancient ruins like the Colosseum and threw coins into the Trevi Fountain. Unfortunately, one rainy afternoon ruined their plans to visit the Vatican Museum, which was extremely disappointing. Despite that setback, both girls agreed it was the best trip of their lives. Companies like Airbnb and Google Maps made planning much easier, and they promised to return to Italy again next year with their families.
